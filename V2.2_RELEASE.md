# V2.2 — Floor Execution

V2.2 extends the Supervisor Command Center without replacing the existing production records or sync format.

## Workflows

- Downtime reports can use measured minutes or a start/stop timer. Timers persist across reloads, split elapsed time across calendar dates and stop when the report is resolved from either Command Center or Today. Reason, planned/unplanned classification and maintenance status are recorded.
- Quality reports add stage, defect, suspected source, containment, disposition, rejected/reworked/replacement quantities and up to two compressed evidence photos. Photos remain part of the existing local, backup and configured sync record.
- Saved handovers snapshot the open report references and require acknowledgement by a selected app user. Editing the saved handover clears its old acknowledgement. The reader can see which referenced items remain open or were resolved afterward.
- The Supervisor report provides a bounded 31-day view of recorded planned/unplanned downtime, unmeasured downtime reports, quality reports and resolved follow-ups. It supports safe CSV export and the existing print/PDF flow. It does not infer historical production targets, OEE or non-overlapping loss from incomplete data.
- Phones gain a persistent Quick entry control for downtime, quality, actions and handover.

## Data boundaries

Maintenance status is documentation; it does not send a maintenance notification. Quality source is explicitly labelled as suspected. Photos are compressed and capped per report and per dataset to protect browser storage. Existing V2.1 reports remain readable and receive sensible defaults only when edited.

## Verification

The existing test suite remains unchanged. New domain and browser checks cover timer persistence and day splitting, duplicate machine timer prevention, completion behavior, quality evidence persistence, carryover and acknowledgement invalidation, CSV formula neutralization, mobile quick entry and report export.
